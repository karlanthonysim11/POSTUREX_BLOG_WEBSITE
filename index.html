About POSTUREX

Meet POSTUREX — Intelligent Sitting-Posture Awareness

POSTUREX is an Internet of Things (IoT)-based prototype developed to support posture awareness among people who spend extended periods seated, particularly office workers and other desk-based users.

The system uses a removable sensor-embedded chair sleeve, an ESP32 microcontroller, and an Edge Impulse TinyML model to identify three project-defined sitting-posture states: upright, slouched, and leaning.

Through Bluetooth Low Energy, the system communicates with a mobile application that provides real-time posture information, monitoring history, analytics, and non-diagnostic AI-assisted coaching.

  • Camera-free
  • Non-wearable
  • Fits existing chairs
  • Non-diagnostic

The Problem

Posture drifts without anyone noticing

People who spend extended periods studying, programming, or working at desks may gradually change their sitting posture without noticing.

Conventional chairs generally lack automated posture monitoring and real-time feedback, and periodic ergonomic assessments only capture a snapshot in time.

Unmonitored sitting over time

The POSTUREX Solution

A chair sleeve that senses, classifies and informs

POSTUREX introduces a removable sensor-equipped chair sleeve that collects sitting-related measurements and uses embedded machine learning to classify defined sitting postures.

The connected mobile application helps users recognize their recorded sitting behavior through real-time monitoring, corrective feedback, history, and analytics.

Key Features

Everything the prototype does, in one workflow

Real-Time Posture Monitoring

View posture classifications during active monitoring sessions, with the device connection state shown in the app.

Embedded TinyML Classification

Uses an Edge Impulse model deployed on the ESP32 to recognize three defined sitting-posture states: upright, slouched and leaning.

Removable Sensor-Embedded Chair Sleeve

Designed for installation on compatible existing chairs without permanent structural modification.

Posture History and Analytics

Review previous sessions and observe recorded sitting-posture patterns through daily, weekly and monthly views.

AI-Assisted Coaching

Receive supplementary, non-diagnostic posture-awareness suggestions based on monitoring summaries.

Mobile Connectivity

Connect the chair-based prototype to the POSTUREX mobile application through Bluetooth Low Energy.

Expert Review

Registered physical therapist: 30/30, acceptable without revision

On October 9, 2026, a registered physical therapist from San Pedro Hospital, Davao City, observed a demonstration and rated six statements on a five-point scale. Every item received 5 (Strongly Agree).

One expert questionnaire supports the suitability of the features for posture awareness. It does not establish diagnostic accuracy, clinical effectiveness or regulatory approval.

  1. Posture categories appropriate for the monitoring purpose5/5
  2. Sensor placement reasonable5/5
  3. Posture alerts understandable for general users5/5
  4. Correction recommendations and AI guidance appropriate and not unsafe5/5
  5. Analytics understandable without implying a diagnosis5/5
  6. Overall appropriate for the academic monitoring purpose5/5
Evaluation scope and limitations
  • Controlled classification used three participants (CT1–CT3); results may not generalize to a larger or more diverse population.
  • The model recognizes only upright, slouched and leaning. Rotated, reclined or transitional postures are not separately classified.
  • FSR readings can change with sleeve shift, clothing, body dimensions and chair geometry; consistent installation matters.
  • The app's live display depends on a working BLE link. No fixed BLE range is claimed.
  • The app was reported working on the Android phones and iPhones the team tested; compatibility is not claimed beyond those.
  • AI-assisted coaching needs its cloud service and is optional, non-diagnostic guidance. It does not classify posture.
